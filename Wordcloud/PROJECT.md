# Klaar voor middelbaar

## Concept

An app concept aimed at helping pupils prepare for the transition from primary school to secondary school.

The app helps make the unknown feel known. Many children are not necessarily afraid of secondary school itself, but of not knowing what to expect. *Klaar voor middelbaar* turns vague worries into concrete, manageable preparation steps.

**Language:** Dutch

## Target users

- **Primary:** Groep 8 pupils (ages 11–12) preparing to start de middelbare school
- **Secondary:** Parents or guardians
- **Additional:** Primary school teachers, mentors, or secondary schools supporting incoming pupils

## Core goals

Make students feel more confident, independent and prepared before starting secondary school, focusing on:

1. Practical preparation
2. Reducing uncertainty around the new school environment
3. Social and emotional readiness

## App sections

### 1. Mijn voorbereiding
A personal overview of what the student has already completed.

### 2. Hoe werkt de middelbare?
Information about practical changes: different teachers, classrooms, subjects, timetables and lockers.

### 3. Slim plannen
Exercises related to homework, agenda use, packing bags and preparing for the next day.

### 4. Sociale situaties
Realistic scenarios about making friends, asking questions, handling mistakes and feeling nervous.

### 5. Checklists
Practical lists for school supplies, the first school day, homework routines and bag packing.

### 6. Oefenen
Interactive quizzes, drag and drop tasks, memory games or small challenges.

### 7. Voor ouders
Guidance for parents on how to support their child during the transition.
